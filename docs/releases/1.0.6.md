Released 2026-10-08.

- **VR Settings:** the entry now occupies the same first-level position in the
  main and pause menus of Overture, Black Plague and Requiem, in English/Spanish.
  Requiem gains its native page, live turn/crouch/height edits and height calibration.
- **Black Plague:** physical use of the initial vice and easier locker acquisition;
  physical-contact step filtering; mixed joystick/HMD movement can negotiate
  eligible movable obstacles without the previous static-only rejection.
- **Requiem:** correct eye height after the intro without opening inventory,
  physical/button crouch, stable post-physics slope height, tracked fullscreen UI
  and persisted turn/crouch settings.
- **Overture:** logic cadence follows the headset refresh rate with a 90 Hz
  fallback; shared visibility covers both asymmetric eyes before per-eye rendering.

[Download PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.6.exe](https://github.com/rubocopter/penumbra_vr_framework/releases/download/v1.0.6/PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.6.exe).
Close the games and run the installer to update. Existing personal settings,
custom bindings and original-file backups are preserved. Matching source,
checksums and the build report accompany the installer.

The maintainer accepted the preceding BP/Requiem feedback fixes; the new shared
menu routes and mixed-obstacle correction have host tests and await scoped
headset confirmation. Overture's new cadence/visibility changes, non-Sense
controller coverage and independent clean-PC acceptance remain separate.
Requiem's intermittent startup crash is still under investigation.

### Español

El acceso a **Ajustes de VR** está en el mismo sitio, en el primer nivel del menú
principal y de pausa de los tres juegos. Requiem incorpora sus ajustes de giro,
agachado y altura, con guardado y aplicación inmediata. Se incluyen las
correcciones validadas del torno y la taquilla de BP y de la altura inicial de
Requiem, además del ajuste para evitar el bloqueo excesivo ante obstáculos
móviles al combinar joystick y movimiento del visor. Los nuevos menús y este
último ajuste aún requieren su confirmación específica en visor.

Cierra los juegos y ejecuta el instalador para actualizar. Se conservan los
ajustes personales, los bindings y las copias originales. El fallo intermitente
de arranque de Requiem sigue bajo investigación.
