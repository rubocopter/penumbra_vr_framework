Released 2026-10-16.

## Black Plague interaction and progression

- Fix contact selection and native Examine/SaveArea routing without extending
  physical grabs through walls or replacing native scripted activation.
- Improve articulated prop acquisition, including the dog/altar sequence,
  bucket/container and handle, and the anchored arm puzzle. Jointed bodies
  retain native constraints, masses, limits and lifecycle.
- Prevent barrels from receiving the extra camera-directed impulse when a
  VR-owned Push is released. Correct offset-center-of-mass hinge motion so the
  wall lever reaches its native puzzle activation threshold.
- Activate fixed computer terminals from their native usable range. Their HPL
  GUI cursor and popup buttons now receive tracked pointer and native mouse
  press/release events, including the incoming-call Answer button.

## Readability and presentation

- Center Examine/gameplay messages horizontally and as a complete vertical
  block, with twice the native font size and line spacing. Preserve native
  wrapping metrics, text, fades, black-text mode and shadow layers.
- Hide equipped flashlight/glowstick geometry during UI rendering and restore
  its exact visibility afterward. Equip and light state are preserved; headset
  confirmation of this presentation change remains separate.
- Keep the existing user SubtitleScale setting and native radio/menu text
  behavior. Include bounded computer pointer/click diagnostics for reports.

The maintainer accepted the focused prop/puzzle corrections, computer reach,
cursor/Answer and enlarged centered text on the local candidates identified in
the [acceptance record](1.0.8-headset-acceptance.json). This does not imply
full-game, other-device or exact new-Setup headset acceptance. Overture and
Requiem have versioned packaging updates with no new gameplay changes.

[Download PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.8.exe](https://github.com/rubocopter/penumbra_vr_framework/releases/download/v1.0.8/PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.8.exe).
Close the games before updating. The installer retains recorded component
selections, original-file backups and settings under its existing ownership
rules. Matching source, dependency sources, SHA-256 checksums and build report
are provided with the installer.

Broader articulated contact, initial dog acquisition, low-obstacle/leaning world
correction, flashlight attachment and equipped-light menu visibility remain
focused regression gates. The physical-only attribution and step-normal changes
have host evidence; they do not close low-obstacle traversal. Requiem's
intermittent startup cause, Index finger feedback and independent clean-PC or
other-controller validation remain open.

## Español

Se corrigen interacciones de Black Plague que impedían avanzar: objetos
articulados y brazos del puzle, barriles que salían disparados al soltarlos,
palancas de pared y el asa del cubo. Se conserva el funcionamiento nativo de
las articulaciones y los eventos de los puzles.

Los ordenadores fijos se pueden activar a su distancia nativa y su cursor y
botones emergentes funcionan con el mando, incluido el aviso de llamada. El
texto al investigar aparece centrado y con letra e interlineado al doble de
tamaño. Estos casos tienen aceptación del mantenedor en los candidatos locales
registrados; no equivalen a validar todo el juego o todos los dispositivos.

Se incluye la ocultación de la luz equipada durante las interfaces, pendiente
de confirmar en visor. Siguen abiertas las pruebas más amplias de contacto,
obstáculos bajos/inclinación física y alineación de la linterna. El fallo
intermitente de arranque de Requiem continúa sin causa corregida demostrada.
Se distribuyen el instalador actualizado, el código fuente correspondiente,
las fuentes de dependencias, las sumas y el informe de compilación.
