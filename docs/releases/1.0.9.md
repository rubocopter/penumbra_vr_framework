## Movement and jumping across the trilogy

- Use a common normal movement profile: **3 m/s walking and 4.5 m/s sprinting**
  at the default movement setting. Keep the physical headset translation bound
  separate from stick travel so low update rates or mixed input do not truncate
  the requested stick movement.
- Connect Requiem's second held-jump query, which could clear a valid VR hold
  and shorten the jump. Preserve native player-state and collision ownership.
- Adapt Overture's normal jump to the independently verified Black Plague/
  Requiem profile across headset update rates. Combine keyboard and controller
  Jump once, preserving native tap/hold, release and airborne repress behavior,
  crouched takeoff and scripted/solver overrides.

## Positional audio, uphill motion and filing cabinets

- Update the Black Plague/Requiem audio listener from the tracked central head
  pose, including physical head turns and room-scale translation. Restore the
  native listener on tracking/focus/presentation loss and audio context changes.
- Bound accepted uphill step advance by the measured surface rise, addressing
  native overshoot on shallow slopes. Keep native collision selection and one
  physics update; do not smooth or offset the camera to hide the motion.
- Allow Black Plague filing-cabinet drawers to leave their sticky closed/open
  endpoint while held by a VR hand. Preserve native joints, travel limits,
  scripts and release restoration.

These corrections respond to a Quest 3 report. Release validation includes
**66 Framework host tests and 298 Overture checks**, full Release builds and
installer/package checks. They do **not** establish new live/headset acceptance.
The reported long-jump platform, perceived audio direction, affected slope/stair
surfaces and cabinet interaction still need user feedback on the public build.
Historical headset acceptance remains scoped to its recorded binaries.
Requiem's intermittent startup issue and existing controller limitations remain.

[Download PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.9.exe](https://github.com/rubocopter/penumbra_vr_framework/releases/download/v1.0.9/PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.9.exe).
Close the games before updating. The unified installer preserves recorded
components, backups and settings under its existing managed ownership rules.
Matching committed source, dependency sources, build provenance and SHA-256
checksums accompany the installer.

For feedback, include the game, Framework version, headset/controllers,
connection/audio route, active bindings, map/save location and reproduction
steps. See the [focused acceptance checklist](../VR_HEADSET_TEST_CHECKLIST.md#trilogy-feedback-candidate--independent-acceptance).

## Español

Se unifica el movimiento normal de la trilogía a **3 m/s caminando y 4,5 m/s
corriendo**. Se corrige el salto mantenido de Requiem y se adapta el salto normal
de Overture al perfil nativo de BP/Requiem a distintas frecuencias del visor,
con una única entrada combinada de teclado y VR.

El audio posicional de BP/Requiem sigue la orientación física de la cabeza. Se
corrige el avance vertical excesivo al subir superficies poco elevadas y los
archivadores de BP permiten tirar con la mano conservando sus juntas y límites.

La publicación tiene comprobaciones automatizadas de host; la plataforma
reportada, el audio percibido, las pendientes y los archivadores están pendientes
de confirmar con visor. Se publica para recoger feedback sin atribuirle una
validación en Quest 3 que todavía no se ha realizado.
