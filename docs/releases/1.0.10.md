## Requiem throwing and interactions

- Assisted throwing now follows the controller holding the object. Aim that
  controller, then press Examine (default **B** in the right-handed Touch
  layout). Left-handed/custom bindings may assign Examine differently.
- Keep the original game's throw power, mass profile, CanBeThrown checks and
  puzzle/script behavior. Ordinary gesture release retains its existing
  Rework velocity gains/caps; invalid focus, UI or stale tracking references
  cannot contribute release momentum.
- Examine and ladder selection now have an independent tracked-head query,
  so their cues can appear while the hand points elsewhere. Examine refreshes
  its head target when pressed. Physical hand interaction, native distances,
  description-once rules, ladder guards and foreground occlusion remain intact.

This maintenance update responds to Caelus's Quest 3 level03 bottle throw and
selection report. The reported walking/jumping improvement and brief favorable
audio observation are scoped feedback without an exact installed hash.
Movement, jumping and audio are unchanged in this update.

All **68 Framework host tests pass**. Regressions cover the actual adapter, native throw ordering and mass
profiles with the shipped Newton DLL, independent head/hand targets and hook
restoration. They do **not** establish level03 completion or headset aiming/cue
usability. Those checks remain open in the [focused checklist](../VR_HEADSET_TEST_CHECKLIST.md#requiem-throw-and-selection--independent-acceptance).
Requiem's intermittent startup crash remains unresolved.

[Download PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.10.exe](https://github.com/rubocopter/penumbra_vr_framework/releases/download/v1.0.10/PenumbraVR-Setup-1.0.10.exe).
Close the games before updating. Matching committed source, dependency sources,

## Español

El lanzamiento asistido de Requiem sigue ahora la dirección del mando que
sostiene el objeto. Apunta con ese mando y pulsa Examinar (**B** en el perfil
Touch diestro predeterminado). Se conservan la potencia y las reglas nativas;
soltar con gesto sigue usando los límites anteriores.

Examinar y las escaleras tienen una consulta independiente desde el visor,
manteniendo las distancias, obstáculos y restricciones del juego. Esto permite
encontrar sus iconos aunque la mano apunte a otro sitio. Al pulsar Examinar se
actualiza el objetivo de la mirada.

Los cambios tienen pruebas automatizadas de host. Completar el puzle de la
botella del nivel03 y confirmar el apuntado y los iconos con Quest 3 sigue
pendiente de feedback. Esta versión conserva el movimiento, salto y audio.
